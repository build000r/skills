# Engagement Voice Guide (Generic)

Use this guide when drafting feed summaries or suggested first-contact replies.

## Principles

1. Lead with context-specific value, not pitch language.
2. One concrete insight per message.
3. Keep claims grounded in the source post.
4. Match tone to platform norms.

## Fast Style Rules

- Reddit: practical and specific; include implementation hints.
- Twitter/X: concise; emphasize one sharp point.
- LinkedIn: professional; tie advice to business outcomes.
- HN: technical depth; avoid marketing phrasing.

## Avoid

- Generic feature dumps.
- Fake urgency.
- Unverifiable claims.
- Copy-paste replies across candidates.

## Quality Check

A draft is acceptable only if:

- It directly addresses the user’s problem.
- It would still be useful with product names removed.
- It contains at most one clear call-to-action.
