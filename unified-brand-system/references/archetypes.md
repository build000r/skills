# Brand Archetypes

Pick the closest match or name a hybrid (e.g. "architectural minimalism + trust finance"). Each archetype implies a default palette direction, type pairing, radius, and density.

## architectural-minimalism

High-contrast black/white/grey. Strong grid, generous whitespace, structural shadows. Feels like a serious building, not a startup.

- Palette: near-black ink on off-white paper; one accent reserved for action only.
- Type: geometric sans display (Inter Display, GT America, Söhne), humanist sans body.
- Radius: sharp (0) or slight (4px). Never pillowy.
- Density: airy. Large type, long line-height.
- Use for: finance, legal, architecture, high-stakes professional services, boomer-legibility constraints.

## warm-papered

Cream background, ink-black text, warm accent. Reads like a printed journal.

- Palette: #efe4d2-adjacent paper, ink #16211b, teal or rust accent.
- Type: serif display (Fraunces, Tiempos), humanist sans body.
- Radius: soft (8px) or pillowy (14px).
- Density: medium.
- Use for: editorial, lifestyle, premium goods, content-heavy.

## technical-industrial

Graphite and amber. Monospaced accents. Feels engineered.

- Palette: dark neutral base, warm signal color, cool secondary.
- Type: grotesk display, mono for emphasis.
- Radius: sharp to slight.
- Density: high.
- Use for: infra, devtools, industrial SaaS.

## luxury-editorial

Black, white, one heavy accent. Large imagery, few words.

- Palette: pure black, pure white, optional deep accent.
- Type: high-contrast serif display (Canela, Ogg), neutral sans body.
- Radius: sharp.
- Density: airy.
- Use for: fashion, beauty, high-ticket goods, art.

## trust-finance

Navy or graphite, ivory, restrained accent. Classic institutional.

- Palette: deep blue or charcoal primary, ivory or cool white background, single gold/teal accent.
- Type: transitional serif display, neutral sans body.
- Radius: slight.
- Density: medium.
- Use for: wealth management, insurance, legacy financial services, HOA/condo finance.

## civic-utility

Blue, white, restrained. Built for legibility first, aesthetics second.

- Palette: federal blue, white, accessible red for alerts.
- Type: open-aperture sans (Public Sans, Source Sans), no display face.
- Radius: slight.
- Density: high.
- Use for: government, public health, compliance.

## friendly-consumer

Rounded, warm, plenty of color. Approachable without being childish.

- Palette: warm neutral base, 2-3 playful accents.
- Type: rounded sans (DM Sans, Nunito), optional display script.
- Radius: pillowy (14px).
- Density: medium.
- Use for: DTC consumer, wellness, education.

## Hybrid Hints

You can combine archetypes, but pick exactly one anchor. Examples:

- **architectural-minimalism + trust-finance**: black/white/grey neutrals with a single navy or teal action accent, geometric sans display, humanist sans body, sharp radius, AAA contrast. This is the right anchor for finance tools that need to feel like a building, not a bank.
- **warm-papered + luxury-editorial**: cream background with near-black ink and a gold accent; high-contrast serif display.
- **technical-industrial + civic-utility**: dark neutral base with a federal blue accent; grotesk display; high density.

Do not combine more than two archetypes. Past that, the brand stops having a point of view.
